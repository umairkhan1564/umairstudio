Why the watermark is a problem

The TikTok watermark (the bouncing logo and username) marks a clip as "downloaded from TikTok," which platforms like Instagram and YouTube quietly deprioritise. If you're reposting or editing, you want a clean file. Removing it after the fact blurs the video — the right move is to download a version without the watermark in the first place.
