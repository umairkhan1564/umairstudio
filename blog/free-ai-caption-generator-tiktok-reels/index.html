Why captions matter so much

Studies of short-form feeds keep showing the same thing: captioned videos hold attention longer and get watched to completion more often. On a muted feed, your words are your video. Word-by-word captions that pop in sync with the voice also add motion, which keeps thumbs from scrolling.

The catch is that typing and timing subtitles by hand is painfully slow. An AI caption generator does it in seconds.

Tips for captions that convert

  • High contrast wins. Bold white with a black outline, or a boxed style, reads on any background.
  • 1–2 words per line. Big, punchy words beat long sentences on a small screen.
  • Match the vibe. Comedy? Try Comic or Bubblegum. Hype? Beast or Neon. Clean talking-head? Minimal.
  • Keep them low but not cut off. Leave room for the platform's UI at the very bottom.
