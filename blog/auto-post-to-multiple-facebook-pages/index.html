How to Auto-Post to Multiple Facebook Pages

Updated July 2026 · 5 min read

Running one Facebook Page is manageable. Running ten, twenty or more by hand is a full-time job. Here's how to schedule and auto-post Reels, videos, stories and photos across many Pages at once — hands-free, from your own PC.

The problem with posting by hand

Page operators and content teams hit the same wall: uploading the same Reel to dozens of Pages, spacing posts so they don't look spammy, and doing it every single day. It's repetitive, easy to mess up, and it doesn't scale. The fix is a tool that posts for you, on a schedule, across every Page you manage.

How to set it up

1

Get the Bulk Uploader

Download the ClipCut Bulk Uploader — a Windows desktop app. It runs on your own PC (or a VPS), so there are no API tokens to manage.

2

Add your accounts

Log in once and the app auto-fetches every Page on that account. Add as many accounts as you run.

3

Point each Page at a folder

Drop your Reels/videos/photos into folders, set how many to post per day, the gap between posts, and where captions come from (file names or a text file).

4

Press Play — it posts for you

The app posts to every Page on your schedule, page by page. Posted files move aside so nothing goes out twice.

Feed it fresh content automatically

The uploader posts whatever is in your folders — so the real bottleneck becomes making enough content. That's where Shortify pairs perfectly: turn one long video into a batch of captioned Shorts, drop them into your posting folders, and let the uploader distribute them across every Page.

Tips to stay safe and effective

  • Space your posts. Use realistic gaps between posts rather than dumping everything at once.
  • Vary captions. Pull captions from file names or a text list so every Page isn't identical.
  • Watch your Page quality. Post original, transformative content — it's what keeps Pages healthy and monetizable.
  • Start small, then scale. Get one account's schedule right before adding the rest.

FAQ

Do I need Facebook API tokens?

No. The app logs in through your own browser session and fetches your Pages automatically.

What can it post?

Reels, videos, stories, image posts and text posts — on a daily schedule.

Does it run in the cloud?

It runs on your own Windows PC or a VPS, so you stay in control.
