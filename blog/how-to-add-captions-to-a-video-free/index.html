Adding captions used to mean typing every line and dragging it to the right timestamp. Now AI does it in seconds. Here's how to auto-caption any video for free — with the captions styled and burned right into the video, no watermark.

Burned-in captions vs. subtitle files

There are two kinds of captions. A subtitle file (.srt) is separate and only shows if the player supports it. Burned-in captions are part of the video itself — they always show, on every platform, styled exactly how you want. For social video (Shorts, Reels, TikTok), you want burned-in captions, because they display even in a muted, autoplaying feed.

Caption styling tips

  • Contrast is everything. White text with a dark outline, or a solid caption box, stays readable over any footage.
  • Fewer words, bigger text. One or two words at a time reads faster than full sentences.
  • Animate for attention. Word-by-word "pop" or karaoke styles add motion that holds the scroll.
  • Stay above the UI. Keep captions off the very bottom edge where platform buttons sit.
