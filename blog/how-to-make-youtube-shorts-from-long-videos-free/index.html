Why turn long videos into Shorts?

Short-form video is where new audiences find you. A single long upload already contains your best moments — the punchlines, the hot takes, the "wait, what?" reactions. Clipping those moments into vertical Shorts lets one piece of content work ten times as hard, and each clip is a fresh chance to go viral and send viewers back to your main channel.

The problem is time. Manually scrubbing a 40-minute video, cutting clips, reframing them to 9:16 and typing captions takes hours. That's exactly what a good shorts maker automates.

Tips to make your Shorts actually go viral

  • Start on a hook. The first 1–2 seconds decide everything. Trim each clip so it opens on the most surprising line, not the setup.
  • Always use captions. Most people watch on mute. Word-by-word captions dramatically boost watch time — pick a bold, high-contrast style.
  • Keep it tight. 15–40 seconds usually outperforms 60. Cut dead air.
  • Post consistently. One long video = 5–10 Shorts = a week of daily posts. Consistency beats perfection.
  • End on a loop or a question. A clip that makes people rewatch or comment gets pushed to more feeds.
