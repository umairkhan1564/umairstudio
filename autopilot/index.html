How it works

Set it up once — then it runs every day.

Install & activate

Download the installer, open AutoPilot and send us your Machine ID (Settings → License) to get your key.

Connect your pages

Add your Meta app or Facebook IDs in Connections, with an IP route for each ID.

Give it content

Paste links or creator profiles in Auto Pipeline, or save them as a daily feed for each page.

Let it run

Posts go out daily or on schedule. Watch the results and insights on the Dashboard.
